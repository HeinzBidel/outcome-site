The ten books of Allan Kardec, unabridged, in our own translation from the original French. Offline, ad-free, no account.

Complete reading for free · illustrations and study as a one-time purchase · in five languages

Everything Rivail has.

Kardec's books to read, hear, pray and study — faithful to what he published.

The library

Kardec's ten books, unabridged.

The Spirits' Book with the 1,018 questions of the 2nd edition, The Gospel According to Spiritism, The Mediums' Book, Heaven and Hell, Genesis and five more works — complete, with no subscription.

The translation

Translated from the French, with the original in view.

Not a single line comes from another translation. The text was translated from the editions Kardec published and checked against period facsimiles. Whenever you want, the original French appears under each item.

Notes on this edition

Where there is doubt, the note says what is printed.

The notes explain period terms and show what the original edition says, so you can read Kardec as he wrote.

Illustrated

A period scene in every chapter.

More than 150 reconstructed scenes, one per chapter, each with the caption that says where what it shows comes from. And the documented covers of each work.

Prayers

Prayers for every moment.

The prayers of The Gospel According to Spiritism, organized by moment: at bedtime, in affliction, for someone who is ill, for those who have died, to ask for counsel.

Gospel at Home

The family gathering, step by step.

The Gospel at Home guide: opening prayer, reading, commentary and closing prayer. A short gathering with the family, on the same day and time each week.

Study of the Doctrine

56 themes: what the ten works say.

God, reincarnation, mediumship, free will, the future life, charity. Three reading plans for The Spirits' Book and search across all ten works at once.

For every day

Light, parchment or dark.

Passage and question of the day, reminder and widget, a card to share with the source cited, text size and color adjustment for color blindness. And The Spirits' Book and the Gospel in audio, offline.

On iPad

Kardec on a big screen.

On iPad, reading gets wide margins and the page reads like a book. In Brazilian and European Portuguese, Spanish, English and the original French.

Reading is free. The text is not for sale.
