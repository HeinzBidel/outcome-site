Privacy is not a feature. It is how we build.

Four commitments apply to every app we publish.

Works offline

The books, the images and the study material live on your device. On a plane, on the road or with no signal, the app opens and you read.

We collect no data

No usage tracking, no analytics tools, no crash reports sent. We don't know what you read, and we don't want to.

What's yours stays with you

Highlights, notes and your progress are saved on your phone. If your phone backup is on, the copy goes to your Google or Apple account — never to us.

No account, no ads

Install and start reading, with no sign-up and no password. No advertising, no advertising identifier.

See for yourself: put your phone in airplane mode and open the app.

Outcome Analytics general policy

Who makes them

Outcome Analytics is a Brazilian studio from Matão, São Paulo. It grew out of a path that runs from the electronics workbench to data engineering: measure, check and fix. That is what we apply to every text and every screen.

Android and iOS apps

From the first prototype to publication in the stores, with on-device testing and content in several languages.

Content checked at the source

Complete texts, translated and checked against the original editions, with notes that say what each detail is based on.

Data and metrics

Data engineering and dashboards that put a name to the problem — without needing the data of the people who use our apps.
