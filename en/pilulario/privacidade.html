Privacy Policy

Pilulário · Last updated: November 23, 2026.

In short

Pilulário has no account, no ads, does not use the internet and does not collect data. What you type stays on your device. Data only leaves the device when you share it, export it or hand it over to another phone yourself.

What the app does

It is a medication alarm and reminder: it alerts you at each dose time and keeps what was marked. It is not a medical device, does not suggest any medicine, dose or time, and does not replace advice from a health professional.

What stays on the device

This data is stored only on your device:

  • names of people and medicines;
  • times and marked doses;
  • stock;
  • notes, allergies and care notes you write;
  • photos you take (profile, box, prescription);
  • SOS numbers.

We have no server and receive none of this data. Also:

  • we ask for no sign-up or account;
  • we show no ads;
  • we use no usage analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools;
  • the app does not access the internet (on Android it does not even have that permission);
  • we do not sell or share data with anyone.

What leaves the device — only when you ask

  • Sharing a summary (today’s, the week’s, or the list for the doctor): you choose the app and the recipient.
  • Exporting the backup: creates a file that you keep or send wherever you want.
  • Caregiver handover: the app shows a code on the screen that another phone reads with its camera; nothing goes through the internet.

System backup: on Android, the app’s data is excluded from Google backup. On iPhone and iPad, if you use iCloud Backup, the device backup includes the app’s data, under Apple’s rules.

Permissions and why

PermissionWhy
Notifications and exact alarmsTo alert you at the right time for each dose, also after the device restarts.
CameraTo read the handover code (no photo is kept) and to take the photos you ask for.
PhotosTo pick a photo from the gallery when you ask.
Phone (Android only)So that SOS calls the number you tap right away and, if you turn on that option, to call the contact person when a dose of an “Alarm” medicine is not marked. Calls are made by the phone itself, to the numbers you entered.

Purchase

The one-time purchase “Family and caregiver” is made through the store (Google Play or App Store), which handles the payment. We receive no card or payment data; the app only learns whether the purchase was made.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

In More › More options › “Delete everything” you delete all data from the device. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything (on iPhone, an earlier iCloud backup may remain until you delete it in iCloud settings).

Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

The app is published by Heinz H. Bidel (Outcome Analytics), in Brazil, the data controller under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Because Pilulário sends us no data, we hold nothing of yours to look up, correct or delete: the data is on your device, and you see, correct, export or delete it yourself in the app.

Even so, you may exercise your rights under the LGPD and the GDPR (confirmation, access, correction, deletion, portability, information and withdrawal of consent; in the European Union, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, also restriction and objection) by writing to our e-mail. We reply within 15 days. You may also complain to Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the data protection authority of your country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this same page, with the date at the top.
