Privacy Policy

Sabormundi · Last updated: November 24, 2026.

In short

Sabormundi has no account, no ads, and collects no data. What you save in the app stays on your device. Data only leaves it when you yourself share, print or export.

What the app does

It is a book of traditional recipes from around the world: each recipe has a photo, ingredients, method, allergens and the story of the dish. Once installed and with the content downloaded, it works without internet.

What stays on the device

This data is stored only on your device:

  • favorites, “want to try”, “made it” and your notes;
  • your own recipes and the photos you add to them;
  • shopping list, week plan, freezer and passport;
  • allergens and restrictions you mark in “My kitchen”;
  • settings: language, text size, colors, units and reminders.

We have no server and receive none of this data. In addition:

  • we do not ask for sign-up or an account;
  • we do not show ads;
  • we do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools;
  • the app makes no internet connections of its own (on Android it does not even have that permission);
  • we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Content and internet

On Android, recipe texts and photos arrive in packs delivered by Google Play, which you choose to download; that download is carried out by Google Play, under Google’s rules. On iPhone, the content comes inside the app itself. Links to recipe sources open in your browser, outside the app, only when you tap them.

What leaves the device — and only when you ask

  • Sharing or printing a recipe or the shopping list: the app hands the page to the system, and you choose where it goes.
  • Exporting the backup: it creates a file that you keep or send wherever you want.

System backup: on Android, if your Google account backup is turned on in the device, it may include the app’s data, under Google’s rules. On iPhone, if you use iCloud Backup, the device backup includes the app’s data, under Apple’s rules. In both cases we have no access to that copy.

Permissions and why

PermissionWhy
Notifications, alarms and vibrationSo the cooking-mode timer rings with the screen off, for the dish of the day (if you turn it on) and for the backup reminder. Everything is scheduled on the device itself.
Start with the device (Android only)To re-arm the reminders you turned on after the phone restarts.
PhotosThe app does not ask for camera or gallery access. When you add a photo to a recipe of your own, the system itself opens the picker, and only the photo you choose is stored in the app.

Voice (read aloud)

Read aloud uses the voices installed on your device. If you choose a voice that needs internet, the recipe text is processed by that voice’s provider (for example, Google), under its rules.

Purchases

Paid plans (monthly or yearly subscription and the one-time “Forever” plan) are sold by the store (Google Play or the App Store), which handles payment, cancellation and refunds. We receive no card or payment data; the app only learns which plan is active.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Allergies and food safety

Each recipe lists its main allergens and safety notes, but brands and ingredients vary. Always check the label of each product and follow the advice of your doctor or dietitian. Sabormundi is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease.

Images

Some images in Sabormundi are illustrations generated by artificial intelligence; the others are freely licensed photographs, with author credit when the license requires it. The illustrations show the dish as it usually looks; yours may turn out different.

Deleting the data

You can delete items inside the app, clear the app’s data in the device settings, or uninstall it. A Google or iCloud backup made earlier may continue to exist until you delete it in your account settings.

Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

The app is published by Heinz H. Bidel (Outcome Analytics), in Brazil, the data controller within the meaning of the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Because Sabormundi sends us no data, we hold nothing of yours to consult, correct or delete: the data is on your device, and you can see, correct, export or delete it yourself in the app.

Even so, you may exercise your rights under the LGPD and the GDPR (confirmation, access, correction, deletion, portability, information and withdrawal of consent; in the European Union, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, also restriction and objection) by writing to our email. We reply within 15 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or with the data protection authority of your country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this same page, with the date at the top.

Contact

Questions, requests or a mistake in a recipe: contato@outcomeanalytics.com.br — Heinz H. Bidel, Brazil.
